How playful should it be?

I swung too far twice — first a rainbow, then something that looks like a banking app. The mistake both times was treating fun and colour as the same thing. They are not. Below is the identical Play screen at three temperatures. Same structure, same diagonals, same number pad — only the personality changes. Point at one and I will build it.

The same screen, three temperatures

What actually makes it fun — none of it is “more colour”

🐒
A character who talks to you

The monkey reacts: cheers a finished line, points at the next box, looks puzzled when a number repeats. One character carries more warmth than nine gradients ever did.

🍬
Chunk and depth

Fat rounded tiles that look pressable, with a hard shadow underneath so they read as physical objects. Kids want to poke things that look like sweets, not rectangles.

⭐
Reward you can collect

Stars instead of a percentage ring, and a sticker earned per level. Progress a child can show someone beats a progress bar.

🎉
Moments, not decoration

The screen stays calm while you think, then goes loud the instant you win — confetti, the grid flipping green, the monkey celebrating. Contrast is what makes a win feel earned.

🔊
Feedback in the hand

Squish on every press, the candy plink you already tuned, and the spatial stereo you added. That is felt, not seen — and it survives on a screen with almost no colour.

My recommendation, across three screens

Balanced. It keeps the structure and the meaning-carrying colour, but adds the chunk, the mascot and the stars. Playful is genuinely fun but tips toward a toddler app — and your audience runs from 10-year-olds to the adults in your group.
