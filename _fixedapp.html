📐 One screen. Nothing scrolls. Everything still there.

Today the app is a long document: title, target, board, keypad, buttons, math logic, level list, credits — all stacked in one column you scroll through. This turns it into a fixed-height app. The board grows and shrinks to fill whatever space is left, long content moves into bottom sheets, and the Learn notes become pages instead of a scroll. Fixed chrome totals about 340px. On a 640px phone the board gets ~300px; on a 900px phone it gets ~560px and simply grows. Nothing is cut, and nothing needs scrolling.

Three techniques doing the work

1 · The board sizes to height, not just width.
This is the actual bug behind the scrolling. Today the cell size is calculated from the screen width alone, so on a short screen a 10×10 board is taller than the phone and the page has to scroll. The fix is one line of arithmetic: take the smaller of the width-derived and height-derived cell size.
2 · Long content moves into bottom sheets.
Offline instructions, the formula sheets, credits, the provenance note and the win screen are rare and wordy. They slide up over the app and scroll inside themselves. The screen behind them never scrolls.
3 · Learn becomes pages, not a wall.
The maths notes are the one thing genuinely too long for a phone screen. Instead of a scroll, they become 4 short pages with dots and a Next — each one fits, and a child reads one idea at a time.
