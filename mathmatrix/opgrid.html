Number Grid

Put 1 to 9 in the white squares, each used once. Every row and every column must make its number. Work left to right — not times-before-plus.

Pick a square, then a number.

    Why this one is real arithmetic. The triangle puzzle teaches you where numbers go. This one can be finished without guessing even once — the solver behind “Show me the maths” does exactly that, and every line it prints is a step you could have made yourself. The moves are: list the ways an equation can be made, keep only digits that appear in some way, and a digit used once is gone everywhere else. That is factor pairs, divisibility and elimination.
